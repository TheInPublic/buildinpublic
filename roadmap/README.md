## What is #buildinpublic

**#BuildinPublic** is the practice of openly sharing the journey of building a product, business, or project. It involves showing progress, setbacks, learnings, and wins in real-time, inviting others to engage, provide feedback, and follow along. 

The goal is to build transparency, connect with an audience, and foster community-driven growth.

## How to Build in Public

This section provides a phased approach to building in public, helping you establish a solid foundation, grow an engaged audience, and effectively share your progress.

---

### Phase 1: Preparation and Planning

#### Define Your "Why"
Establish the core purpose of building in public for your project:
- **Transparency & Trust**: Establish authentic credibility from day one.
- **Early Audience**: Generate interest and attract early adopters before launch.
- **Accountability**: Stay motivated and disciplined through public commitment.
- **Co-Creation**: Build a community-driven product shaped by real feedback.

#### Set Clear Goals
- **Audience Growth**: Target follower, subscriber, or newsletter benchmarks within specific timeframes.
- **Product Milestones**: Define deliverables and shipping deadlines.
- **Engagement Metrics**: Track comments, reshares, feature votes, and community inputs.

#### Choose Your Primary Platforms
- **X (Twitter)**: Ideal for rapid micro-updates, casual thoughts, polls, and instant maker interactions.
- **LinkedIn**: Excellent for professional networking, B2B reflections, and founder storytelling.
- **Personal Blog / Substack**: Suited for long-form essays, monthly transparency reports, and architectural deep-dives.
- **YouTube / Loom**: Great for screen recordings, video walkthroughs, and behind-the-scenes vlogs.

#### Develop a Content Cadence
- **Daily Updates**: Small wins, completed tasks, hurdles overcome, or quick code snippets.
- **Weekly Recaps**: Highlight key progress, metrics changes, and weekly takeaways.
- **Monthly Deep Dives**: Analyze revenue growth, user metrics, and strategic reflections.

---

### Phase 2: Building an Audience

#### Establish Your Narrative
- **Introduce Your Journey**: Clearly explain why you are building your product and the core problem you aim to solve.
- **Share Your Origin Story**: Discuss the events that led you here—your background, struggles, and aspirations.

#### Engage and Connect
- **Respond Active to Comments**: Treat every reply as a conversation starter to foster community ties.
- **Ask Direct Questions**: Involve your audience in product decisions (e.g., UI designs, pricing tiers, naming).
- **Amplify Fellow Builders**: Support other makers by liking, commenting, and sharing their milestones.

#### Utilize Hashtags & Niche Networks
- Use `#buildinpublic` to join the global movement of builders.
- Combine with domain-specific tags: `#saas`, `#nocode`, `#indiehacker`, `#ai`, `#webdev`.

#### Build Relationships & Collaborations
- Connect directly with peers through DMs, Twitter Spaces, co-authored articles, or podcast interviews.

---

### Phase 3: Sharing Progress Effectively

#### Consistency is Key
- Maintain a structured content schedule while keeping building as your primary job.
- **Balance Sharing and Building**: Avoid spending excessive time crafting content at the expense of shipping code.

#### Show, Don’t Tell
- **Visual Proof**: Embed screenshots, short screen-recordings, GIFs, or flowcharts.
- **Share Failures Openly**: Embrace public setbacks—audiences respect honesty and resilience over artificial perfection.

#### Invite Continuous Feedback
- **Conduct Polls**: Gather input on feature prioritization or UI aesthetics.
- **Share Early MVPs**: Provide private beta links to active supporters for early feedback.

---

### Phase 4: Engaging the Community

#### Launch Challenges and Incentives
- **Building Challenges**: Participate in or host mini-building sprints (e.g., 24-hour hackathons, 30-day shipping challenges).
- **Early-Bird Perks**: Offer lifetime discounts or exclusive beta access to reward active supporters.

#### Host Interactive Sessions
- **Live Streams & Spaces**: Host live Q&As or live-coding streams.
- **AMA (Ask Me Anything)**: Invite followers to ask about your tech stack, business strategy, or founder journey.

#### Celebrate Wins Together
- **Publicly Acknowledge Milestones**: Share major revenue achievements, user milestones, or tech achievements.
- **Highlight Community Contributions**: Express gratitude to supporters who report bugs or offer valuable suggestions.

---

### Phase 5: Metrics, Reflection, and Adjustment

#### Track Key Metrics
- **Audience Growth**: Track subscriber count, follower growth rate, and impressions.
- **Engagement Quality**: Measure reply depth, shares, and DM conversations.
- **Product KPIs**: Monitor sign-ups, active retention, MRR, and conversion rates.

#### Reflect and Adapt
- **Monthly Reflection Posts**: Write transparent posts analyzing what worked, what failed, and key learnings.
- **Pivot When Necessary**: Building in public allows you to adapt quickly if an assumption proves wrong. Double down on content formats and product features that resonate most.

---

### Phase 6: Launch and Post-Launch

#### Launch Publicly
- **Build Pre-Launch Momentum**: Run a countdown campaign leading up to launch day.
- **Tell the Full Story**: Share the backstory—why you started, the obstacles overcome, and the final result.
- **Multi-Platform Launch**: Launch on Product Hunt, Hacker News, Reddit, X, and relevant developer forums.

#### Post-Launch Engagement
- **Showcase Customer Success**: Share testimonials and user case studies.
- **Maintain Communication**: Continue sharing new features, security updates, and upcoming roadmap items.
- **Monetize Ethically**: Offer early-bird deals and exclusive community perks for your core supporters.
