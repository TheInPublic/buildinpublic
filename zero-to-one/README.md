# Zero to One: Ideation, Problem Discovery & Market Validation

> **A first-principles guide to discovering real user problems, conducting customer interviews, and validating product ideas before writing code.**

---

## 1. Finding Hair-on-Fire Problems

Great software products are built to eliminate painful friction. Avoid "solutions looking for a problem." Instead, search for **hair-on-fire problems**—issues so painful that users are currently paying for clunky workarounds or cobbling together spreadsheets to solve them.

```text
┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                      THE PROBLEM DISCOVERY FILTER                         │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
   1. Is the problem Painful?    ──► (Saves high time or money)
   2. Is the problem Frequent?   ──► (Occurs daily or weekly)
   3. Is the market Willing to Pay? ──► (B2B workflow vs casual entertainment)
```

---

## 2. Customer Interview Validation: The Mom Test Methodology

Created by Rob Fitzpatrick, **The Mom Test** is a set of rules for talking to potential customers so that even your mom can't lie to you about whether your idea is good.

```text
┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                          THE MOM TEST RULES                               │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
   Rule 1: Talk about THEIR life & existing behavior, not your hypothetical idea.
   Rule 2: Ask about SPECIFIC events in the past, not opinions about the future.
   Rule 3: Talk LESS and listen MORE.
```

### Bad Questions vs Good Questions

| Bad Question (Hypothetical & Leading) | Good Question (Behavioral & Past Evidence) |
| :--- | :--- |
| *"Would you buy an app that automatically cleans your inbox?"* | *"How do you currently manage your inbox? How much time did you spend on it yesterday?"* |
| *"How much would you pay for a tool like this?"* | *"What tools have you paid for in the past 6 months to solve this problem?"* |
| *"Do you think this feature is cool?"* | *"Walk me through the last time that problem caused you to miss a deadline."* |

## 3. Smoke Testing & Landing Page Pre-Validation

Before spending weeks coding full software infrastructure, validate market demand using a **Smoke Test**:

```text
Landing Page (Clear Value Prop) ──► "Join Waitlist" / "Pre-Order" CTA ──► Measure Conversion Rate
```

1. **Build a 1-Page Landing Page**: Clearly define the value proposition and core features.
2. **Set Up a Call to Action (CTA)**: Include a *"Request Early Access"* or *"Pre-order for $29"* button.
3. **Drive Targeted Traffic**: Share on niche developer forums, Reddit, or run a small $50 ad campaign.
4. **Evaluate Metrics**: A conversion rate $> 10\%$ on waitlist signups indicates strong initial intent.

---

## 4. Summary

Validating ideas before writing code saves months of wasted engineering effort. By asking the right behavioral questions and testing demand with lightweight smoke tests, you enter product development with high confidence.
