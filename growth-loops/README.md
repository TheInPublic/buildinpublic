# Growth Loops & Viral Distribution Engines for Indie Products

> **A first-principles guide to replacing traditional linear marketing funnels with compounding growth loops, viral mechanics, and product-led growth (PLG).**

---

## 1. Linear Funnels vs. Compounding Growth Loops

Traditional marketing operates as a **linear funnel**: you pour money or effort into top-of-funnel traffic, a small percentage converts, and the process stops.

A **Growth Loop** is a self-reinforcing system where the output of one user action automatically generates input for new user acquisition.

```text
[ Linear Funnel ]   ──► Spend $ / Ads ──► Traffic ──► Conversion ──► End of Process
[ Growth Loop   ]   ──► New User ──► Uses Product ──► Generates Output/Invite ──► Attracts Next User
```

---

## 2. 3 High-Impact Growth Loop Models for Indie SaaS

### Model 1: Organic Content & Output Loops
Occurs when users create shareable content using your software that naturally displays your product branding or value.
- *Examples*: Figma public design links, Typeform survey pages, Loom video share URLs, Notion public pages.
- *Mechanism*: Every shareable link acts as a live product demo for non-users.

### Model 2: Viral Referral & Invitation Loops
Occurs when core software features require inviting teammates or external collaborators.
- *Examples*: Slack team invites, Cal.com scheduling links, Documenso contract signature requests.
- *K-Factor Formula*: If each active user invites an average of $> 1.0$ new active users, the product achieves organic viral growth.

### Model 3: Product-Led Incentivized Loops
Users earn product upgrades, extra storage, or credits by inviting peers.
- *Example*: Dropbox giving 500MB free storage for both the inviter and invitee.

## 3. Designing Product-Led Growth (PLG) Mechanics

```text
┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                      PRODUCT-LED GROWTH (PLG) ENGINE                      │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
   Frictionless Signup ──► Instant Time-to-Value ──► In-App Conversion ──► Built-in Sharing
```

1. **Remove Friction at Signup**: Allow users to experience value before forcing long form inputs or credit card collection.
2. **Embed Sharing Triggers at Key Moments**: Prompt users to share their achievements (e.g., publishing a site, shipping a feature) right after experiencing success.

---

## 4. Summary

Growth loops convert your product into its own primary marketing channel. By embedding viral, content, or collaborative loops directly into software workflows, indie creators achieve compounding organic growth.
